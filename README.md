# 🏠 Home/Read Me

## Welcome to the Open-Source Additive Manufacturing Lab

We are a community-driven, grassroots R\&D initiative dedicated to exploring the sides of technology that mainstream commercial manufacturing leaves behind. Operating out of a decentralized "micro-lab" framework, we focus on hacking open hardware, experimenting with custom paste extrusion, and pushing the boundaries of 3D toolpaths.

### Our Core Principles

1. **Radical Transparency:** We publish our failures as openly as our successes. A well-documented clog tells us more than an accidental perfect print.
2. **Open-Source Currency:** Every modification, G-code tweak, and material recipe we develop is shared under a Creative Commons license for public good.
3. **Hardware Agility:** We prove that high-level engineering research doesn't require multi-million dollar facilities—it can begin on a desktop, in an apartment, or in a student garage.

### Active Projects

* **The Ender-3 Hacking Platform:** Converting stock consumer mechanics into robust, modular paste and engineering-grade filament testbeds.
* **The Research Ledger:** A standardized, peer-reviewed logging system for material rheology and failure analysis.
