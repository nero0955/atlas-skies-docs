# 🔬 How to Contribute

## How to Submit a Research Log

Thank you for contributing to the Atlas Skies open-source research initiative! To keep our platform 100% free and open to everyone, we use GitHub to process community data submissions.

You do not need to know how to code or use terminal commands—the entire process happens right in your web browser.

***

#### Step 1: Launch the Editor

Click the button below. It will automatically open a blank file creator directly inside our shared data repository.

[\[ 🔬 Click Here to Open the GitHub Submission Link \]](https://github.com/nero0955/atlas-skies-docs/new/main)

***

#### Step 2: Fill Out Your Data

1. **Name Your File:** In the box at the very top that says `Name your file...`, type a unique name ending in `.md` (e.g., `Clay-Ender3-v1.md`).
2. **Paste the Template:** Copy our standardized research log template, paste it into the large empty text window, and fill in your metrics, recipes, and results.

***

#### Step 3: Propose the Changes

1. Scroll to the very bottom of the GitHub page.
2. Click the green **Propose changes** button.
3. On the next screen, confirm by clicking **Create pull request**.

Once our lab review team verifies the formatting, your log will be merged and will automatically populate live right here on the Atlas Skies Docs website!
