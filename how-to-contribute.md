# 🔬 How to Contribute

## How to Submit a Research Log

Thank you for contributing to the Atlas Skies open-source research initiative! To keep our platform 100% free and open to everyone, we use GitHub to process community data submissions.

You do not need a GitBook account to contribute to Atlas Skies Docs! We run entirely on open-source GitHub infrastructure to ensure our research remains decentralized and accessible.

***

### Pre-Requisites

Before you begin, ensure you have a free account created at [GitHub.com](https://github.com). You will need to be logged in to submit your data.

***

### Step 1: Launch the Editor

Click the direct button below. It will automatically bypass the repository main screen and open a brand-new, blank file creator right inside our shared data folder.

[\[ 🔬 Click Here to Open the GitHub Submission Link \]](https://github.com/nero0955/atlas-skies-docs/new/main)

***

### Step 2: Paste & Name Your Log

Once the GitHub link opens, follow these simple steps right in your browser:

1. **Name Your File:** In the box at the very top that says `Name your file...`, type a unique name ending in `.md` (e.g., `Clay-Ender3-v1.md`).
2. **Paste the Template:** Copy our standardized research log template, paste it into the large empty text window, and fill out your specific printer metrics, material recipes, or test results.

***

### Step 3: Propose Changes

1. Scroll to the very bottom of the GitHub page.
2. Click the green **Propose changes** button. _(GitHub will automatically create a secure fork behind the scenes)._
3. On the final screen, confirm your submission by clicking **Create pull request**.

### What Happens Next?

Once our lab review team verifies the formatting of your submission, your log will be merged into the main repository. GitBook's automated pipeline will then instantly push your research live onto the Atlas Skies website!
